First Aid Instructions

Quick reference guides for common emergency situations

Severe Bleeding

Critical emergency requiring immediate action

  1. 1
    Apply direct pressure

    Use a clean cloth or bandage and press firmly on the wound.

  2. 2
    Elevate the injured area

    Keep the wound above heart level if possible.

  3. 3
    Do not remove the bandage

    If blood soaks through, add more layers on top.

  4. 4
    Call emergency services

    Dial 911 or your local emergency number immediately.

Burns Treatment

Thermal, chemical, and electrical burn care

  1. 1
    Cool the burn

    Run cool (not cold) water over the burn for 10-20 minutes.

  2. 2
    Remove tight items

    Remove rings, watches before swelling begins.

  3. 3
    Cover loosely

    Use a sterile, non-stick bandage or clean cloth.

  4. 4
    Don't break blisters

    Breaking blisters can lead to infection.

Choking Response

Heimlich maneuver and airway clearance

  1. 1
    Assess the situation

    Ask "Are you choking?" If they can speak or cough, encourage them to continue.

  2. 2
    Perform abdominal thrusts

    Stand behind, wrap arms around waist, make a fist above navel.

  3. 3
    Thrust inward and upward

    Repeat until the object is expelled or person becomes unconscious.

  4. 4
    If unconscious, begin CPR

    Call 911 and start CPR if the person loses consciousness.

Heart Attack

Recognizing and responding to cardiac emergencies

  1. 1
    Call 911 immediately

    Time is critical. Don't wait to see if symptoms go away.

  2. 2
    Keep the person calm

    Have them sit in a comfortable position, loosen tight clothing.

  3. 3
    Chew aspirin if available

    If not allergic, chew and swallow one adult aspirin (325mg).

  4. 4
    Be ready for CPR

    If person stops breathing, begin CPR immediately.

Bone Fractures

Immobilization and care for broken bones

  1. 1
    Don't move the injured area

    Keep the limb as still as possible to prevent further damage.

  2. 2
    Apply ice

    Use an ice pack wrapped in cloth to reduce swelling.

  3. 3
    Immobilize with splint

    Pad the splint and secure above and below the fracture.

  4. 4
    Seek medical help

    Go to the ER or call for ambulance transport.

What To Do In An Emergency

Stay calm and follow these steps to handle any emergency situation

Take a deep breath and quickly evaluate the situation. Determine if there's immediate danger to you or others. Your calm demeanor will help others stay focused and follow instructions.

Dial emergency services (911, 112, or your local emergency number). Provide your exact location, nature of emergency, number of people involved, and any known medical conditions. Stay on the line until the dispatcher tells you to hang up.

Remove any hazards if safe to do so. Turn off vehicles, move flammable materials, clear paths for emergency responders. If the scene is dangerous, move to a safe distance and wait for professional help.

If trained, provide appropriate first aid. Control bleeding, maintain airway, treat for shock. Only move an injured person if they're in immediate danger. Keep the person warm and reassure them that help is coming.

Don't leave the scene until emergency services arrive. Be ready to guide them to the exact location. Provide any relevant information about what happened and any first aid already administered.
